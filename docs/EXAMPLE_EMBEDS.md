## Wording rules the builder enforces

- Every link is built from the exact mint (DexScreener uses the selected pool address).
- Market cap shows `UNAVAILABLE (FDV shown below, not market cap)` when the provider lacks it.
- UNKNOWN fields stay `UNKNOWN`. Sample-based numbers state the sample size.
- The risk label always includes `NOT RUG-PROOF`. INCOMPLETE risk is never published.
- The footer always says it is a research signal, not a trade recommendation, and that realized profit is unknown.
